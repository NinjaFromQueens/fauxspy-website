How the App Would Work

No switching apps. No copying links. You'd share the photo and get your answer, from anywhere you can browse photos on your iPhone or Android.

Browsing a dating profile, social media feed, or news article and something looks a little too perfect? A little too smooth? That's when you need Faux Spy.
