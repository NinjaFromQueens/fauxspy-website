How it works

  1. In your browser: right-click any image and choose "Investigate this image".
  2. On your phone: screenshot the photo, crop it to just the picture, and upload it above or at fauxspy.com/check.
  3. Get a verdict (AI-generated, real photo, digital art or inconclusive) with a confidence score, in a few seconds.

No detector is perfect, including ours. Treat a result as one strong signal alongside where the photo came from. Faux Spy doesn't store the images you check.
