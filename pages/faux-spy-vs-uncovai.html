Deepfake detection: the use case UncovAI doesn't document

There are two distinct scenarios where AI detection matters for personal safety:

Fully AI-generated faces: Photos created from scratch by tools like Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion. These are entirely synthetic — there's no real person behind the face.

AI-manipulated real photos (deepfakes): Real photos of real people that have been altered using AI — face-swapped, aged, retouched. Scammers often use this approach because it passes basic visual inspection and doesn't trigger reverse image search hits.

Faux Spy Pro includes a dedicated deepfake detection model that looks specifically for AI manipulation in otherwise authentic photos. UncovAI's capabilities on this second scenario aren't documented. For romance scam protection — which often involves manipulated real photos rather than fully synthetic ones — the distinction matters.
