The problem with a completely free extension

BitMind is genuinely free — no subscription, no paid tier, no revenue. For occasional use that sounds like a pure win. But there's a structural problem: AI image generators improve constantly, and keeping detection models current against Midjourney v7, Flux, DALL-E 3, and whatever launches next requires real investment.

A product with no revenue model has no budget to retrain. That means accuracy against current generators may decline over time, even if the extension still works fine against older ones. Scammers use the newest generators — they're not still running Stable Diffusion v1 from 2022. A detection tool stuck on older training data will miss them.

Faux Spy charges $9.99/month for Pro because that subscription funds the API that continuously retrains against new generators as they're released. The free tier (3 checks/day) still exists and still gets the same detection models — the subscription funds the backend that keeps it accurate.

Deepfake detection: the gap that matters for romance scams

Most romance scam catfishing doesn't use fully AI-generated faces. Scammers often use real photos that have been lightly AI-manipulated — face-swapped, aged, or retouched. That's the deepfake scenario, and it's different from detecting a fully AI-generated image.

Faux Spy's Pro tier includes deepfake detection using a separate model that looks specifically for signs of AI manipulation in otherwise real photos. BitMind's detection capabilities aren't fully published — its primary positioning is around AI-generated image detection, not deepfake manipulation detection specifically.

If you're checking profile photos on a dating app and want to catch both types — fully synthetic faces and real-but-manipulated photos — Faux Spy Pro covers both cases.

What BitMind does well

BitMind's unlimited free tier is a genuine advantage for users who want basic AI detection without any cost or daily limit. If you're doing occasional checks and don't need deepfake detection or video scanning, it's a reasonable starting point.

The extension installs from the Chrome Web Store and works passively — it can flag AI-generated images as you browse without requiring manual right-click interaction on each image.

When Faux Spy is the right choice

Faux Spy makes sense if you're actively checking profile photos on dating apps, social media, or anywhere you're evaluating whether a person is real. The right-click UX keeps you on the page you're already browsing — no uploading, no navigating away. The free tier (3/day) handles most regular use without payment.

Pro ($9.99/month) adds deepfake detection — critical for romance scam scenarios — plus unlimited checks and the confidence that paid subscribers are funding ongoing model accuracy. Video scanning ($29.99/month) adds AI detection for video content including TikTok, YouTube, and video call frames.

Common questions

Is BitMind actually free forever?

As of 2026, BitMind has no paid plan and is entirely free. There's no guarantee that continues indefinitely — a free product without a revenue model is structurally dependent on its founders' ongoing willingness to absorb costs. Faux Spy's revenue model makes its continued development more predictable.

Does Faux Spy have an unlimited free option?

Faux Spy's free tier is 3 checks per day — no account required. That covers most typical use. Pro adds unlimited checks for $9.99/month. BitMind offers unlimited free checks, which is an advantage for high-volume use at no cost.

Which is more accurate — Faux Spy or BitMind?

Faux Spy uses a commercial API that is continuously retrained against new generators as they release. BitMind's detection model and update cadence aren't publicly documented. Independent benchmarks on current generators (Midjourney v7, Flux Dev, DALL-E 3) aren't available for BitMind specifically.

Can I use both extensions at the same time?

Yes — Chrome extensions don't conflict with each other. Some users run both: BitMind for passive background detection while browsing, and Faux Spy for deliberate right-click checks on specific profile photos they want to investigate carefully.

Does BitMind scan videos?

No. BitMind focuses on still images. Faux Spy's Video tier ($29.99/month) adds AI detection for video content — useful for checking deepfake videos on social media and verifying whether video call participants are who they claim to be.
