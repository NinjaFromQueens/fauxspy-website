The deepfake gap: why it matters for catfishing

There are two types of fake profile photos you might encounter. The first type is fully AI-generated — a face synthesized from scratch by Midjourney, DALL-E, or a similar tool. These have never existed in the real world. The second type is a deepfake — a real photo of a real person that's been AI-manipulated: face-swapped onto a different body, aged or de-aged, skin retouched, or expression altered.

Both Faux Spy and Fake Image Detector can flag the first type. Only Faux Spy Pro catches the second. The deepfake model uses a different signal — it's looking for artifacts introduced by AI manipulation, not for the signature of a fully generated image. Those are distinct problems requiring distinct detection approaches.

In romance scam scenarios, the manipulated-real-photo approach is extremely common. Scammers take real photos of military personnel, doctors, or attractive strangers and lightly alter them to avoid reverse image search matches. A tool that only detects fully AI-generated images won't catch these.

Development velocity and free-only limitations

Fake Image Detector is free with no paid plan. That means no revenue to fund model updates as AI generators improve. Midjourney alone has gone through seven major versions — each one producing images that look significantly more photorealistic than the last. A detection model trained primarily on older generators will struggle with current output.

Faux Spy's Pro subscription funds the commercial API that continuously retrains detection models against new generators as they release. The free tier (10 checks/day) uses the same updated models — the subscription funds the infrastructure that keeps them current.

What Fake Image Detector does well

For basic AI-generated image detection at no cost, Fake Image Detector is a functional tool. It works as a Chrome extension and doesn't require you to upload images to an external site. If you need occasional basic detection and deepfake identification isn't part of your use case, it covers that scenario.
