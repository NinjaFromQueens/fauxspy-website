Quick Answer

Faux Spy is a free browser extension for Chrome, Firefox and Edge that tells you whether a photo or video is AI-generated. Right-click any image — on Tinder, Instagram, Facebook or anywhere else — and get a verdict with a confidence score in about two seconds. It's built to help people spot fake dating profiles, catfish and romance scammers who use AI-generated photos.

FAQ

AI image detection FAQ

How does Faux Spy detect AI-generated images?

Faux Spy sends the image URL to two AI-detection engines, Hive and Sightengine, and also checks the image for camera provenance (C2PA content credentials) and EXIF metadata. You get a confidence percentage, not a flat yes/no, so you can judge edge cases yourself.

How can I tell if a dating profile photo is AI-generated?

Right-click the photo and run Faux Spy on it. AI-generated faces often look flawless but carry pixel-level patterns your eye can't see, which detection models pick up. Also watch for other warning signs: very few photos, a relationship that moves fast toward money, and excuses to avoid video calls.

Is Faux Spy free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 3 scans per day with no account or credit card. Paid plans start at $9.99/month for 200 scans and come with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Which AI image generators can it detect?

Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Adobe Firefly, Leonardo AI and most other major generators. Detection of newer generators improves as the underlying models are updated.

Does it work on Instagram and other social media?

Yes. It works on Instagram, Pinterest, X, Reddit, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads and any other site you open in your browser. The only exception is an image whose URL sits behind a login the detector can't reach.

Can I use it to check photos on dating apps like Tinder or Bumble?

Yes. Open Tinder, Bumble or Hinge in your browser, hover over a profile photo and click Investigate. Checking dating profile photos is one of the most common reasons people install Faux Spy.

Is it accurate?

It's a strong signal, but no detector is 100% accurate. Photos from major generators like Midjourney, DALL-E and Stable Diffusion are the easiest to catch; newer generators and heavily compressed images are harder. That's why every result comes with a confidence percentage: treat a high score as a strong warning and a borderline score as a reason to look closer.

What should I do if the result says "Inconclusive"?

Treat it as "not enough signal", not as "real". Inconclusive results are more likely on compressed thumbnails, images resaved many times, or unusual angles. Try scanning a larger version of the photo, and weigh the result alongside other warning signs.

What data does Faux Spy collect?

Very little. When you scan, the image URL is sent to our detection API. We don't store your images, track your browsing history, or collect personal information.

How do scans work? What happens when I run out?

Each image scan uses one scan. Free users get 3 per day, resetting every 24 hours with no signup. Secret Agent and Master Spy subscribers get 200 scans per month, refreshed on the billing date. Extra scan packs (50, 200 or 500) can be bought inside the extension and never expire.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account dashboard at any time and keep Pro access until the end of your billing period. If you're not happy, email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.
